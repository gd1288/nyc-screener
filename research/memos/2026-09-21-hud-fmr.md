# HUD Fair Market Rents (FMR)

**Candidate file:** `research/candidates/hud-fmr.json`
**Verdict: recommend_with_caveat**

## What it is
HUD's official 40th-percentile gross-rent estimate, published annually for every US county and
~4,700-4,800 HUD metro-FMR areas. Used to set Housing Choice Voucher payment standards, but it's
public federal data usable for any purpose. Accessed via a token-gated API
(https://www.huduser.gov/portal/dataset/fmr-api.html) — free account signup, same pattern as this
project's existing `CENSUS_API_KEY` requirement.

## Why it could help
Zillow ZORI (`zillow_research.py`) is the only rent series in the project today, at ZIP-level with
coverage that thins out fast outside Zillow's core metros. FMR is a national, county-level rent
series that would backstop `gross_rent_yield` (neighborhood.py) and the rent assumptions feeding
`scoring/investment.py` wherever ZORI is missing or sparse.

## Evaluation
- **Coverage/granularity:** national, county/metro-FMR-area level. Every US county has a value,
  unlike ZORI's patchy ZIP coverage outside major metros.
- **Cost/license:** free, public-domain federal data. Requires a free account + token, not a
  meaningfully higher bar than the project's existing Census API key.
- **History:** FY2018–present via the API, longer as static files. Shorter than FHFA HPI or
  Freddie Mac's mortgage series but adequate for a current-level input rather than a growth series.
- **Correlation:** not computed (`evaluate_series.py` doesn't exist yet). Worth flagging: FMR is
  the 40th percentile of gross rent for standard-quality units (a voucher-program statistic), not
  a median asking-rent figure like ZORI — the two measure genuinely different things even in areas
  where both exist, so this isn't a like-for-like swap.

## Recommendation
Recommend with a caveat similar to the FEMA NRI candidate: it's a real national rent series with a
clean license, but for NYC condos specifically it doesn't clearly beat the Zillow ZORI already in
place — different percentile statistic, no obvious edge for the current use case — and adding it
now would mean a second, partially-overlapping rent adapter with modest marginal value. Its real
case is as the national fallback once this project covers areas outside Zillow's footprint. Worth
having on record; not urgent to build today.
