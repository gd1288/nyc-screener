# FHFA House Price Index — metro/state purchase-only index

**Recommend (metro/state level only).**

## What it is
FHFA's repeat-sales House Price Index, built from mortgage-transaction data obtained from Fannie
Mae/Freddie Mac. The official purchase-only index exists for the nation, census divisions, states,
and Metropolitan Statistical Areas/Divisions, quarterly, since 1975, as free CSV/TXT/XLSX
downloads — no login, no key.

## Why it could help
`backend/app/scoring/backtest.py`'s own docstring frames "FHFA tract HPI growth" as the ground
truth the growth score should predict — but no FHFA data is actually ingested anywhere in this
codebase today. Both the momentum pillar and the backtest currently rely solely on Zillow Research
ZHVI, a single private estimate-based index, and `app/sources/zillow_research.py` additionally
hard-filters that feed to NYC counties only. FHFA HPI is methodologically independent (actual
repeat-sales transactions, not an estimate model) and is a genuinely national series — the
strongest available building block for this project's stated goal of generalizing the screener
beyond NYC.

## Evaluation
- **Granularity**: official MSA and state indexes are non-developmental and CSV. I checked FHFA's
  datasets page and its local-HPI FAQ directly (via search, since `fhfa.gov` wasn't reachable from
  this session to fetch): there is **no official county-level CSV**. The one county file that
  exists (`hpi_at_bdl_county.xlsx`) is explicitly labeled **developmental** by FHFA itself and is
  XLSX, not CSV. A census-tract "developmental" file also exists. Treat the backtest docstring's
  mention of "FHFA tract HPI growth" as aspirational, not something actually available at
  production quality.
- **Update frequency**: quarterly (official); annual (developmental county/tract).
- **History**: MSA purchase-only index since 1975 — far longer than Zillow ZHVI's own
  history, useful for a more convincing backtest.
- **Cost/license**: free, US government work, public domain, no key, no rate limit.
- **Quantitative evaluation**: not run — `app/research/evaluate_series.py` doesn't exist yet.
  Once it does, the natural first test is correlating FHFA MSA HPI growth against Zillow ZHVI
  growth for the NYC metro, as a sanity check before trusting either series to extrapolate to a
  metro with no Zillow coverage.

## Recommendation
Implement at the metro/state level. This is the single best candidate in this batch for the
stated "any US neighborhood" goal, precisely because it doesn't depend on Zillow's NYC-only CSV
filtering. Don't build on the county/tract files — FHFA's own developmental label means they're
not reliable enough to anchor the momentum pillar or backtest on.
