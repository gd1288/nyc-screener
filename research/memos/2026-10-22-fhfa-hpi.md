# FHFA House Price Index (HPI)

**Recommendation: recommend**

## What it is
The Federal Housing Finance Agency's repeat-sales House Price Index, built from actual conforming
mortgage transactions. Published quarterly at national/census-division/state/MSA level, and
annually at county, 5-digit ZIP, and census-tract level (FHFA calls the smaller geographies
"developmental" — noisier, sometimes suppressed for low-volume areas). Free static file downloads
(CSV/JSON/XML/XLSX) at fhfa.gov/data/hpi/datasets — no documented REST API was found, no account
needed either way.

## Why it could help
This project's own `research-sources` skill already names the evaluation target it expects a new
candidate to be checked against: "correlation and lead/lag of the candidate series against FHFA
tract HPI growth and Zillow ZORI." But grepping the codebase turns up no FHFA source at all —
`zillow_research.py` is the only appreciation series in the pipeline today.

I read `backend/app/scoring/backtest.py` to check this rather than assume it: it builds both its
price-pillar predictors (valuation gap, momentum) *and* the outcome it scores them against
entirely from Zillow ZHVI history (`hist_zhvi_*` metrics pulled by `zillow_research.py`). Per its
own docstring, the non-price pillars (permits, census, crime) "aren't backtested yet" at all. So
right now there is exactly one data series in the whole validation loop, feeding both sides of the
predictor/outcome split, and no independently-sourced appreciation benchmark anywhere in the
project.

## Evaluation
- **Granularity**: county and ZIP5 annual files fit this project's neighborhood-level ambitions
  reasonably well; tract-level CSV exists too but is the thinnest/noisiest cut, so I'd prefer
  county or ZIP for anything sold as a scored input.
- **Cost/license**: free, no login, no restriction found — US government data.
- **History**: decades at the state/national level; shorter but still usable at county/ZIP/tract.
- **Quantitative evaluation**: not run — the evaluation tooling doesn't exist yet. This candidate
  is actually a prerequisite for that tooling to mean anything (it's the second benchmark series
  the design calls for), not something to be evaluated against it.

## Recommendation
Recommend. This fills a specific, already-identified gap in the project's own validation design,
not just a generic "more data" pitch. Build it as a secondary appreciation benchmark alongside
Zillow, preferring the county-level file over tract where FHFA flags low reliability, rather than
swapping it in as the primary series.
