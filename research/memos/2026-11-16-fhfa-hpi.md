# FHFA House Price Index (HPI)

**Recommendation: recommend-with-caveats** (rank #2 of 3 this run)

## What it is

FHFA's repeat-sales house price index, built from conforming mortgages. Published as a long-running
quarterly series at national/state/MSA level (back to 1975), plus newer experimental annual files at
county and census-tract level ("local HPI").

## Why it could help

Two separate reasons:

1. **The research-sources skill assumes this data already exists, and it doesn't.** `SKILL.md` step
   4 says to benchmark new candidates against "FHFA tract HPI growth and Zillow ZORI." A grep of
   `backend/` for `fhfa` found zero matches — there is no FHFA ingestion anywhere in this codebase.
   The benchmark the skill's own evaluation step depends on doesn't exist yet. That's worth flagging
   on its own, independent of whether this specific candidate is approved.
2. **Standalone value as a second opinion on appreciation.** This project already ingests Zillow's
   ZHVI (`zillow_research.py`) for neighborhood home values. FHFA's HPI is methodologically
   different (repeat-sales index vs. Zillow's estimated-value index) and has far longer history
   (1975 vs. Zillow's ~1996 start). An independent appreciation series is useful both as a sanity
   check on ZHVI-derived growth numbers and as backtest ground truth for the Growth Score once
   `backtest.py`/`evaluate_series.py` exist.

## Evaluation

- **Geographic granularity:** the long-running quarterly all-transactions series is state/MSA level;
  the newer "local HPI" files add county and census-tract, but only annually and (per search
  snippets) as `.xlsx` rather than CSV for the county file — a different parsing path than this
  project's other CSV-based sources.
- **Cost:** free; no login found for the standard downloads (CSV/JSON/XML/SQL per FHFA's own
  datasets page). No API key needed, unlike FRED/Census/BLS.
- **License:** not confirmed from a primary source this run — `www.fhfa.gov` is outside this
  session's allowed network hosts (see Network note). FHFA describes its indexes as "the only
  public, freely available house price indexes" covering all states, which reads as open, and
  federal government work product defaults to public domain (17 U.S.C. §105) — but that's an
  inference, not a direct read of FHFA's terms page. **Confirm directly before implementing.**
- **History:** quarterly national index back to 1975; state/MSA coverage generally starts in the
  1980s and varies by area; the county/tract local-HPI files are a shorter, newer series.
- **Correlation:** not computed (`evaluate_series.py` doesn't exist). Also doesn't quite apply in
  the usual sense — this candidate's primary proposed role is *as* the benchmark other candidates
  get tested against, not a predictor to be benchmarked itself.

## Verdict

Recommend, with caveats: this closes a real gap between what the project's own research procedure
assumes exists and what's actually implemented, and adds genuine independent signal. Before
implementing, (1) read FHFA's terms directly rather than relying on secondhand snippets, and (2)
decide between the long quarterly state/MSA series (CSV, longer history, coarser geography — best
fit as backtest ground truth) and the annual county/tract files (finer geography, shorter history,
Excel format — best fit as an extra neighborhood-growth metric), likely implementing both for their
different jobs.
