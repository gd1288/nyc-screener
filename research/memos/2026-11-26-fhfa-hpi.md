# FHFA House Price Index (HPI), tract/developmental tier — DO NOT RECOMMEND (yet)

**What it is:** FHFA's repeat-sales house price index, built from conforming mortgage data.
The authoritative, fully-vetted product is quarterly at MSA/state/national level, back to 1975.
FHFA also publishes an annual tract-level "developmental" HPI, which it explicitly labels
experimental rather than production-grade.

**Why it was considered:** the research-sources skill's own evaluation step names "FHFA tract HPI
growth" specifically as a backtest benchmark to check candidate signals against. So the product
actually worth weighing here is the tract-level developmental index — it maps to NTA via the same
tract-to-NTA crosswalk `census_acs.py` already uses for ACS data, not the coarser quarterly MSA
series. (An earlier pass of this memo argued against the MSA product on granularity grounds; that
was the wrong target — the tract product isn't coarse, so that argument doesn't hold. The real
issues are below.)

**Evaluation:**
- FHFA itself calls this tier "developmental"/experimental, which is a real caveat against
  treating it as ground truth anywhere the Growth Score or backtest needs a reliable number.
- It's published annually, versus the fully-vetted MSA series' quarterly cadence and versus
  Zillow ZHVI's monthly cadence — a lower-frequency series even where it is trustworthy.
- Its only stated use in this project, per the skill itself, is as a backtest-validation input —
  it isn't a Growth Score signal. It has nothing to validate against until
  `app/research/evaluate_series.py` (Phase 4) actually exists; right now there's no consumer for
  it at all.
- The project's existing backtest ground truth — `dof_condo_sales` (actual closed NYC condo sales)
  plus Zillow ZHVI at ZIP/NTA granularity — is already finer-grained and production-grade, so this
  wouldn't close a gap those don't already cover.
- Quantitative correlation: not computed — Phase 4 doesn't exist yet.

**Recommendation: do not recommend for now**, specifically because there is no backtest
infrastructure yet to consume it and the only NTA-matching version of this data is FHFA's own
experimental tier. Revisit once Phase 4 (`evaluate_series.py`) is built and a second, independent
backtest benchmark is actually wanted alongside Zillow/DOF sales.
